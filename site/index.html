One record in, every screen out

Change an event in src/data.ts — a city, an hour, a device — and all four templates re-derive themselves: signal weights fire or don't, the haversine travel check runs again, and the score, verdict, stamps and checklist update everywhere. In the sample night, a Lagos sign-in 41 minutes after a Bengaluru one demands 11,913 km/h — so the kit says what a good security team would: it wasn't you travelling; it was your password, somewhere it should never have been.

Latchkey is fictional. Sample data — all names, devices, IP addresses and events are invented; IPs use documentation ranges.
